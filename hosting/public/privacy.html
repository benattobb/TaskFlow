Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

What TaskFlow does

TaskFlow is an Android app for capturing personal tasks. It can optionally create matching items in the Google Tasks and Google Calendar accounts that you choose.

Information processed

Task text, dates, times, recurrence choices, and focus-task information are processed on your device. Natural-language parsing happens locally. TaskFlow does not run its own servers, sell data, show advertising, or use analytics.

When you choose Connect Google and add a task with sync enabled, TaskFlow sends the task title and relevant schedule information to Google Tasks. Dated tasks are also sent to Google Calendar to create an event in your primary calendar. Google then handles that information under the privacy policy for your Google account.

Google permissions

TaskFlow requests permission to create and manage your Google Tasks, and to create Calendar events. It does not read your Calendar data. You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions.

Storage and security

Focus-task information stays in TaskFlow's app-private storage. Android backups and clear-text network traffic are disabled. Google access tokens are obtained on demand from Google Play services and are not written to TaskFlow storage or logs.

Your choices

You can use local capture without connecting Google, disconnect Google access through your Google account, and uninstall the app to remove its on-device data.
